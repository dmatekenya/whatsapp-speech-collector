# QC calibration recording protocol

Record each assigned clip as a WhatsApp voice note. Wait for the next task
before recording. Use **Re-record** if you want another attempt at the current
task. The bot keeps previous attempts.

## Table 1. Recording conditions

| Code | Condition | Setup | Clips per speaker |
|---|---|---|---:|
| C1 | Good | In a quiet room, hold your phone as you normally would and speak clearly. | 3 |
| C2 | Too quiet | Hold your phone normally and speak very quietly. | 3 |
| C3 | Arm's length | Hold the phone at arm's length (about 60–70 cm) and speak normally. | 3 |
| C4 | Background voices | Speak while people are talking in the background. | 3 |
| C5 | Background music | Speak while music is playing in the background. | 3 |
| C6 | Noisy surroundings | Speak with other background noise nearby. | 3 |
| C7 | Echo | Speak in a room where your voice echoes. | 3 |
| C8 | Muffled microphone | Keep the microphone partly covered while you speak. | 3 |
| C9 | Silence in a quiet room | In a quiet room, record without speaking. | 3 |
| C10 | Silence with speech | Record without speaking while people talk in the background. | 3 |
| | **Total** | | **30** |

For C1–C8, record the fixed sentence, answer the fixed conversation starter,
then record free speech. For C9 and C10, record three silence clips of 10–15
seconds each.

See the [study methodology](methodology.md) for the planned recording totals.
