# QC calibration methodology

The study collects voice notes under the ten {ref}`recording conditions <table-1-recording-conditions>`
to calibrate audio-quality checks. The requested condition describes the setup.
Raters assess the clips independently.

C3 tests ordinary speech with the phone held at arm's length (about 60–70 cm).
The speaker can read the screen and records the same three tasks as in C1–C8.
Existing C3 conversation-starter and free-speech clips stay in the dataset.
For enrollments with prior C3 progress, the restored sentence remains due until
recorded. Stored C3 slot IDs remain stable even though the sentence is shown first.
