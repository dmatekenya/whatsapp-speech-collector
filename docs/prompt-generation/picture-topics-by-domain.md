# Picture topics by domain (draft for review)

Nov 17, 2026 · @Dunstan

## How the list is used

This draft proposes **74 topics** across the 12 domains, about 6 per domain. It starts from the topics the manual already names in each domain's scope (Table 2, 58 in total), drops a few that can't be seen in a picture (for example *saving* or *language practices*), and adds common scenes that need their own entry (for example *fetching water* or *mosquito nets*).

**The same list applies to sentences and conversation starters.** One list keeps topics comparable across all three prompt types and lets topic coverage be counted in one place. Scope points left out for pictures because they can't be seen are still covered for text: *saving* by `money-saving`, *language practices* by `heritage`, and *local decisions* by `community-meetings`. The labelling steps are the same for text, with step 1 asking whether the sentence or starter is usable as a prompt.

Annotators label each picture in three steps:

1. **Usable?** Yes or no. If no, give a reason (not relevant, poor quality, visible text, sensitive, duplicate, other) and stop.
2. **Domain:** one of the 12 domains. If none fits, choose **No domain fits**; the picture is then excluded, but the choice is recorded.
3. **Topic:** only the chosen domain's topics are shown, plus **Other (describe)** with a short note. If no topic fits, that's a hint to try a neighbouring domain first.

Topic keys are lowercase with hyphens, like the domain keys. Recurring *Other* notes become new topics at the next review of the list.

## Boundary rules

The topic lists settle most of the confusions seen in the domain evaluation. Where a scene could fit two topics in different domains, choose by the central activity:

| Scene | Topic | Not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Selling food at a market | `market-selling` (work-trade) | `food-dishes` |
| Cooking or eating at home | `cooking`, `eating-meal` (food) | `household-chores` |
| Fetching water at a borehole | `water-points` (public-services) | `household-chores` |
| Carrying water or goods along a road | `walking-carrying` (transport) | `water-points` |
| Children playing a game | `childrens-games` (sport-leisure) | `family-time` |
| Children just sitting or walking together | `friends-neighbours` (family) | `childrens-games` |
| Children walking to school | `going-to-school` (education) | `walking-carrying` |
| Fishing boats being used to fish | `fishing` (agriculture) | `boats-ferries` |
| Drying fish to eat or store | `preserving-food` (food) | `fishing` |
| Making crafts to sell | `crafts-trades` (work-trade) | `heritage` |
| Ceremonial or traditional dance | `traditional-dance` (culture) | `dancing` |
| Church choir | `religious-life` (culture) | `music` |
| Radio used to listen to music for fun | `music` (sport-leisure) | `radio-tv` |
| Neighbours helping each other | `community-cooperation` (family) | `community-meetings` |
| Meeting called by a chief or official | `community-meetings` (public-services) | `community-cooperation` |
| Building a school or clinic | `public-works` (public-services) | `construction-labour` |
| Private building or brick making | `construction-labour` (work-trade) | `public-works` |
| Rubbish or tree cutting | `waste-environment` (nature) | `household-chores` |

## Before editing the manual

- [ ] Review each domain's topics: rename, merge, drop or add.
- [ ] Settle the boundary rules above, especially community cooperation vs community meetings, and private vs public construction.
- [ ] Decide whether funerals stay under *Ceremonies and rites* or are always flagged as sensitive, since the picture content rules currently flag funerals and grief.
- [ ] Decide whether political events (rallies, campaigns) get a topic, or stay flagged until the political-content decision.
- [ ] Pilot: relabel about 50 pictures with domain + topic and compare speed and agreement with the earlier domain-only labels.

Once agreed, the list goes into `config/domains.yaml` (used by the labelling page, the review platform and the models) and into the manual's domain table.
