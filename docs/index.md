# Chichewa Speech Project

This book documents the design, preparation, collection, transcription and quality assurance of the Chichewa speech dataset collected through Tiyankhuleni.

Start with {doc}`methodology` for the combined research methodology, or use the component chapters for day-to-day work. Each component owns its methodology and a separate QA protocol. Platform architecture and operational instructions are maintained separately.

## Document status

Existing methods have been migrated from the original methodology. Prompt-generation source manuals and the human transcription manual are pending; their pages explicitly mark missing material. The contributor onboarding manual is converted from the supplied Word document. Historical plans describe their status at the time, and do not override current methodology or configuration.

## Maintaining the book

See {doc}`publishing` for editing, validation and automatic publication. No participant registry, recordings or environment files are included in the book.
